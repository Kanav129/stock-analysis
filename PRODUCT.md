# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user is a solo personal investor. They open the desk around market hours or when deciding a trade, with their own holdings and watchlist already in the system. The job is to leave with a clearer buy / hold / sell call — not to browse markets for entertainment.

## Product Purpose

A private personal stock analysis desk: sync news and prices, generate research reports, and produce AI ratings that support concrete portfolio decisions. Success is finishing a decision loop with evidence (rating, score, report) without re-running work that already completed today.

## Positioning

One private pipeline from data sync → report → rating you can trust and resume.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, dense, terminal — and fast, direct, no-nonsense. Voice is terse and operational: status, progress, and the call. Emotionally: confident control over a personal workflow, not hype or celebration.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS analytics dashboards — purple gradients, identical card grids, and fluffy “insights” marketing chrome that dilute a trading desk.

## Design Principles

1. **Pipeline over polish theater** — Every surface should make sync → analysis → decision progress obvious and resumable.
2. **Density with hierarchy** — Show more data than a consumer app, but keep the next action (sync, analyze, open ticker) unmistakable.
3. **Decision artifacts first** — Ratings, scores, and report conclusions outrank decorative market chrome.
4. **Operational honesty** — Loading, cancel, resume, and failure states tell the truth; never fake “live” or “done.”
5. **Terminal speed** — Prefer immediate paint and quiet background refresh over waiting for a perfect first payload.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: keyboard operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast for primary UI text, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. No formal WCAG audit required for this personal tool.
