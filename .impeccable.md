## Design Context

### Users
Single user (personal investing dashboard). Reviews positions and AI ratings, typically evening / post-market close on a Mac.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, trustworthy. A personal "trading-desk cockpit" — not a flashy fintech landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Dark. Near-black neutrals tinted toward deep slate-blue (OKLCH).
- **Typography:** Schibsted Grotesk (display), Hanken Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (tabular numerics).
- **Color:** Classic finance semantics — muted green up, muted red down. Single confident accent. 60/30/10 visual weight.
- **Anti-patterns:** No card-in-card, no left-border accent stripes, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no neon cyan/purple AI palette.

### Design Principles
1. Data-first density with breathing room — every number earns its place.
2. Tabular numerics everywhere money appears.
3. Restrained color so accent and gain/loss cues carry meaning.
4. Empty states teach the interface, not just say "nothing here."
5. Optimistic UI for watchlist mutations; empty portfolio states point to the watchlist.
